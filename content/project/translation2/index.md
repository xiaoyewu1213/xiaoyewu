'The Musician at Work' is a biography written by Korean indie musician Kim Mok-In. He tells his life and work stories as a singer songwriter, revealing how he writes lyrics and melodies, makes EPs and prepares live performance in his daily life.

This project is still ongoing and I will keep updating here. Have a read to get closer into a musician's fun life!
