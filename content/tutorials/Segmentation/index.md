This is a brief tutorial based on the book Principles of Phonetic Segmentation written by Pavel Machac and Radek Skarnitzl in 2009. It is organised based on different combinations of segments, providing quick guidelines for phoneticians to place boundaries to distinguish neighbouring sounds.
